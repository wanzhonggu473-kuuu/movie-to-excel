# Product principles

Use these principles to evaluate features and resolve product tradeoffs.

## Positioning

Movie to Excel is a private local movie archive. Its purpose is to help people preserve their own viewing history in an ordinary file they control. It is not a review community or social network.

## Principles

1. **Local-first:** The Excel workbook is the authoritative record and remains on the user's device unless the user chooses otherwise.
2. **Private by default:** Ratings and notes are personal fields. Never publish or share them automatically.
3. **No account required:** Core use must not depend on registration, profiles, followers, or identity services.
4. **No lock-in:** Output must remain usable in ordinary spreadsheet software and portable without the plugin.
5. **No social pressure:** Do not add likes, popularity rankings, public comments, feeds, or follower mechanics to the core product.
6. **User-controlled:** The user chooses the workbook location, presentation layout, language, date format, and whether to use online lookup.
7. **Honest connectivity:** The archive is local, but metadata lookup and poster identification may require internet access. Do not describe those operations as offline.

## Feature test

Prefer a proposed feature when it improves private recording, retrieval, organization, portability, or long-term ownership. Defer or reject it when its main purpose is public engagement, platform retention, advertising, or social comparison.
